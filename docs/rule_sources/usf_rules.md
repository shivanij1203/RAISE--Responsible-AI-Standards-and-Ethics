# USF (Tampa) rules on faculty and staff use of generative AI

Source notes for RAISE rule encoding. All pages accessed 2026-10-30.

How to read this file:
- Every quote below comes from a page opened this session. Quotes are short excerpts, copied as returned by the fetch tool. Where a quote is a fragment of a longer sentence, it is marked "(fragment)".
- "Page date" is what the page itself shows. Many USF pages show only "Copyright © 2026" in the footer, which is a site-wide year, not an update date. Those are marked "no date shown".
- Some USF documents (box.com policy PDFs, the Data Governance Charter PDF, the IRB "additional information" page) could not be opened. They are listed under "Not found / unclear".

Bottom line for RAISE:
1. USF publishes one clearly supported AI tool for faculty and staff: Microsoft Copilot Chat (signed in with a USF account), plus an optional paid Microsoft 365 Copilot (Premium) license bought by departments.
2. USF names three data classification levels (Restricted, Sensitive, Public) but no public page opened this session defines them or maps any AI tool to a level.
3. USF's AI data rule, as published, is a prohibition aimed at public tools: do not share student data, employment data, or other protected or sensitive information; keep complying with FERPA and HIPAA.
4. Teaching guidance is permissive: AI use is the instructor's choice, sample syllabus policies exist, no required syllabus language exists yet, and USF says AI detection is not reliable.
5. A Florida Board of Governors amendment (Regulation 1.001 (3)(o)) will require each university board of trustees to adopt AI syllabus disclosure policies. As of 2026-10-30 it is noticed but not finally adopted. The next Board meeting is Nov 12 to 13, 2026.

## Not found / unclear

1. Definitions of Restricted, Sensitive, and Public. Only the names were found. The Data Governance Charter PDF (https://www.usf.edu/data-governance/documents/data-gov-charter_2026-05-04.pdf) blocked access (robots disallowed). USF Policy 0-507 Data Management and 0-515 Protection of Electronic Personal Information sit on usf.app.box.com and could not be opened. The ISSP standards list is on internal SharePoint.
2. Which classification FERPA student records fall under. One IT page calls "student information" "Sensitive data", but no formal mapping was found. Do not encode FERPA = Restricted or FERPA = Sensitive without confirming with USF IT or Data Governance.
3. Which data levels Copilot Chat or M365 Copilot may hold. No USF page says, for example, "Copilot is approved for Sensitive data". RAISE should treat Copilot as "USF-protected, level not published".
4. ChatGPT Edu, Gemini, Zoom AI Companion, Claude: no USF (Tampa) page found. Search results for these were University of San Francisco pages.
5. Turnitin AI writing detection: whether it is enabled at USF is not stated. Only the general line that AI detection "is not reliably possible with technology" was found.
6. IRB / HRPP AI guidance: none found on the public IRB pages. The IRB "additional information" page (https://www.usf.edu/research-innovation/research-integrity-compliance/ric-programs/irb/irb-hrpp/additional-information/index.aspx) returned a server error. Guidance may exist inside BullsIRB (irb.research.usf.edu), which was not opened.
7. BOG Regulation 1.001 (3)(o): final approval date and effective date not published. Watch the Nov 12 to 13, 2026 Board meeting agenda. A deadline for each board of trustees to adopt its policy was not found.
8. USF Regulation 3.027: the copy opened is a 2020 version on a test host and has no AI language. The current official copy (https://usf.app.box.com/v/usfregulation3027) could not be opened.
9. Page dates: most USF pages (genai.usf.edu, IT, Data Governance, IRB) show no update date. The Confluence Copilot page shows "Updated Apr 20" without a year.
10. Naming drift: the 2023 PDF says "Bing Chat Enterprise Preview", the hub says "Copilot Enterprise" and "Copilot Chat", the IT page says "Copilot Chat (Basic)". These appear to be the same protected Microsoft tool under successive names.
